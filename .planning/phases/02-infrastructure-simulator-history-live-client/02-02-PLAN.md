  <action>
  Replacement mode: given a historical (complete, Vickrey) match and a target bot id, our Policy takes the target's seat. Replay supports auctionType Vickrey only; skip non-Vickrey matches and count them. Field max bid per auction (excluding the target) is EXACT: if the historical winner != target ⇒ field = topBid (winner's bid); if winner == target ⇒ field = paid (runner-up's bid; when paid == 1 the runner-up may not exist, so conservatively use field = 1); no-bid auctions ⇒ field = 0. We win iff our bid > field; at equality the tie is resolved by option tieMode: "coin" (default; seeded rng, win with p = 1/2) or "historical" (equality wins iff the target historically won this auction). Vickrey price = field (or 1 if field == 0). Our balance/words evolve via our policy; other bots' words and scores are replayed from history (their balances from history). Our starting rack = the target's revealed hidden tiles (letters it played but never won) topped up to startingRackSize by sampling from the remaining unknown hidden pool (seeded).
  Decision checks: apply decision.words in order; each must be dictionary-valid and feasible from our current rack (else count rejectedWords and skip); then the bid must be an integer >= 0 and <= our balance after the word rewards (else count rejectedBids and treat it as 0).
  Events: feed our MatchTracker the public events via applyEvent. Result winnerId = our id when we won. It = the historical winnerId when the historical winner was not the target and we lost. It = null with paid 0 when the target historically won and we lost (the runner-up is unidentifiable; count unattributedLosses per match). Never credit our seat with a letter our policy did not win. Event order per auction k (canonical order from load.ts): open(k) → our decide → opponents' historical words with auctionIndex == k and !inClose → result(k) → inClose words of k (sim words after a win) (no look-ahead: opponents' words played during auction k are not visible to our decide for k). Report tracker diagnostics (hiddenOverflow, unseenNegative) per match; they are caused by opponents replaying words with letters we took, are expected, and are not errors.
