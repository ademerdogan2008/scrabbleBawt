  <action>
  Replacement mode: given a historical match and a target bot id, our Policy takes the target's seat. Field max bid per auction (excluding the target) is EXACT: if the historical winner != target ⇒ field = topBid (winner's bid); if winner == target ⇒ field = paid (runner-up's bid; field = 0 when paid == 1 and ambiguous → treat as 1 = reserve) ; no-bid auctions ⇒ field = 0. We win iff our bid > field (tie at equality → coin flip with seeded rng); Vickrey price = field (or 1 if field == 0); our balance/words evolve via our policy; other bots' words and scores are replayed from history (their balances from history). Our starting rack = the target's revealed hidden tiles (letters it played but never won) topped up to startingRackSize by sampling from the remaining unknown hidden pool (seeded). Feed our MatchTracker the public events (results with US as winner when we won; otherwise historical winner), opponents' historical words at their auctionIndex. Deadline/latency ignored (record decide times).
  Report per match: our score, our rank vs. the other bots' HISTORICAL final scores (conservative: they keep letters we took), target's original score/rank, letters won, avg price, unplayed tiles; aggregate: win rate (ours vs original target's), avg rank, avg score, Wilson CIs. CLI: `npx tsx scripts/history-replay.ts --policy starter --target Bron --matches 500 --data <dir> [--real6] [--recent]`.
  Tests: replaying with a "mimic" policy that bids exactly the target's historical top bid when it won (and 0 otherwise) and replays its historical words reproduces the target's original score exactly on sample matches (harness correctness check).
